# Privacy Policy — Compass Plus

Compass Plus is designed to improve the appearance and usability of the Compass Education web interface.

## Data Collection

Compass Plus does not collect, sell, transmit, or share personal information with the developer or any third party.

The extension does not include analytics, tracking scripts, advertising, or telemetry.

## Access to Compass Pages

Compass Plus runs on Compass Education webpages in order to provide its features.

Some features need to read and modify information already displayed on the page. For example, the extension may inspect timetable elements, calendar elements, navigation controls, and other parts of the Compass interface so it can restyle or enhance them.

This processing happens locally in the user's browser.

Compass Plus does not attempt to bypass Compass permissions or provide access to information that the signed-in user would not normally be able to access.

## Locally Stored Data

Compass Plus stores some settings locally on the user's device, including preferences such as:

- Theme and colour settings
- Wallpaper settings
- Uploaded wallpapers
- Interface preferences
- Custom shortcuts
- Other Compass Plus settings

Depending on the feature, this information may be stored using browser local storage or IndexedDB.

This locally stored information is not sent to the developer.

## Wallpapers

Uploaded static or live wallpapers are processed and stored locally in the user's browser.

Compass Plus does not upload these files to an external server.

## Clipboard

When the user chooses the "Copy share link" option, Compass Plus may write the Compass Plus Chrome Web Store link to the clipboard.

Compass Plus does not read the contents of the user's clipboard.

## External Links

Some optional buttons may open external websites, including:

- The Compass Plus Chrome Web Store listing
- The Compass Plus feedback form hosted using Microsoft Forms

Visiting these websites is optional and their own privacy policies apply once they are opened.

Compass Plus does not automatically send Compass page information to these websites.

## Authentication

Compass Plus does not collect or store Compass usernames or passwords.

Authentication continues to be handled by Compass Education and the user's school.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when Compass Plus gains new features or if its data-handling behaviour changes.

Any updated version will be published on this page.
